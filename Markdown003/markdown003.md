# Key Moments in the History of the Internet
![Map](Images/Map.gif)

    From the cold war to the web
#### The internet gerew out of the Cold war fears and the need to keep communications open during a possible nuclear attack. Over time, government research, networking experiments, and new software helped transform Arpanet into the foundation of te modern internet.

| Year | Event | Importance |
| ---- | ----- | ---------- |
| 1957 | Sputnik 1 launched | The Soviet Union launched Sputnik, increasing U.S. fears during the Space Race
| 1958 | ARPA created | The United States created ARPA t support advanced research and defense projects
| 1969 | ARPANET established | ARPANET linked four universities and became the foundation for the modern internet
| 1971 | First email sent | Ray Tomlinson sent the first email and helped poularize the use of the @ symbol
| 1972 | TCP/IP developed | Vinton Cerf and Robert Kahn developed the communication protocols that made network connections more reliable
| 1984 | internet is born | ARPANET split into MILNET and ARPANET, marking a major step in the birth of the internet
| 1989 | World Wide Web invented | Tim Berners-Lee invented the world wide web
| 1991 | Web goes public | The world wide web became avalible to the public
